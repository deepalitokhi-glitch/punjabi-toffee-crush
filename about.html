About Us - Punjabi Toffee Crush

Punjabi Toffee Crush is a free online puzzle game made in India. We are a small independent game development team from Punjab, India. Our goal is to create simple, fun, and lightweight games that everyone can play on mobile without downloading.

Why we made this game? We saw many heavy games need high-end phones. Our game works on low-end phones, loads fast, and is 100% free. It has 100+ levels, daily rewards, toffee bomb boosters, rainbow toffee, and offline mode.

This game is inspired by Punjabi culture - colorful, sweet, and full of joy. The game is safe for kids and adults. We respect your privacy and do not collect any personal information.
